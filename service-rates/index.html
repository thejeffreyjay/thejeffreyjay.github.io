THE ONBOARDING FLOW - HOW IT WORKS

Step 01
SEND AN INQUIRY

Book a discovery call, or send your details first by email or the intake form.

Step 02
DISCOVERY CALL

We go over the tasks you want handed off, the tools you already use, and when you want to start. I tell you whether they can do the job and which option fits: a monthly automation package or hourly support.

Step 03
AGREEMENT & ONBOARDING

You sign a contractor agreement and fill out an onboarding form. I ask only for the access the job needs.

Step 04
WORK AND FEEDBACK

For builds, I test the workflow and send a video walkthrough. For admin work, I log each task and send you an update daily and weekly through email or in your team chat.

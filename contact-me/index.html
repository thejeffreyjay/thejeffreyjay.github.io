REFERENCE NOTES

Do you work part-time or full-time?
Both. Ongoing VA & Automation Support is available for 20, 30, or 40 hours per week at $10–$12/hour. Administrative-only support is also available at $7/hour.
What do you charge?
Ongoing VA & Automation Support: $10–$12/hour. Administrative Support: $7/hour. Starter Automation: from $150 one-time. Standard Automation: from $300 one-time. Advanced Automation: custom quotation. Workflow Maintenance: $125/month for up to 5 hours, with additional support at $25/hour upon approval. Trust & Safety Support is quoted based on requirements.
Do you offer project-based automation?
Yes. I build one-time workflow automation projects based on the client's requirements. Each project starts with a discussion, followed by an agreed scope, deliverables, estimated timeline, and quotation before development.
Do you accept clients from other countries?
Yes. I’m open to working with clients from anywhere in the world, not just the US and AU. Feel free to message me to discuss your location, preferred working hours, and requirements.
How do you handle my data?
I ask only for the access a job needs, and you can revoke it at any time. I also recommend using client-owned accounts for connected applications and services.
Are software subscriptions included?
No. Clients cover their own third-party subscriptions, API credits, hosting, databases, and other paid services. These costs are separate from my service fees.
What is your work setup?
MacBook Air M4 with 16GB RAM as primary, and a Windows 11 laptop with Core i5 and 16GB RAM as backup. I use high-speed internet with a mobile data backup.
What is your availability?
10 PM to 10 AM Philippine time (UTC+8), covering US daytime and Australian morning schedules. I can discuss adjustments based on the client's timeline.
What happens if an automation fails?
Depending on the workflow, an error notification can be sent through Slack or Telegram. Failed executions are then inspected manually to identify and address the issue. Automatic retries or recovery are not guaranteed.

AI & WORKFLOW AUTOMATION

Email Triage & Draft Replies

Incoming emails are labeled and summarized, and a draft reply is saved for approval. Urgent emails trigger an alert, and reply rules are kept in a spreadsheet.

Lead Capture, Scoring & CRM Setup

Booking tools and forms are connected to a CRM or spreadsheet. Each new booking is logged, the lead receives a welcome email, and an alert is sent. AI scores each lead from the booking form answers, and high-scoring leads are marked as priority.

AI Agentic Workflows

AI assistants converse with customers, answer questions from the business’s own documents, validate contact details, and route qualified consultation requests.

Finance & Compliance Automations

Incoming documents are processed and checked against compliance rules using AI. Tasks are assigned and draft messages are prepared based on custom risk criteria.

Website Uptime & Health Monitoring

A live website is checked daily for errors. Status reports and urgent down alerts are sent to the team’s message channels.

AI Content Repurposing

New material from RSS feeds, blogs, and podcasts is turned into social posts for each platform using AI, then distributed across channels.

Order, Booking & Form Alerts

New orders, bookings, and form responses send an alert to the right person or team channel as they happen. For online stores, customers also receive an emailed receipt.

Pre-Call Briefs

AI writes a short brief for each booked call, covering what the person needs and what to prepare. The brief is sent as a chat message and saved in a spreadsheet.

Session & Call Reminders

Clients receive an email reminder at a set time before each booked session or call. The reminder can include what to prepare.

Reports & Data Logging

Each new booking is logged in a spreadsheet. A weekly summary reports total bookings, high-priority bookings, and the average lead score.

OTHER SERVICES: OPERATIONS & ADMIN

Inbox Management

I sort email by priority, flag urgent messages, clear spam, and draft replies for your review, maintaining an Inbox Zero standard.

Calendar Management

I book and reschedule meetings, resolve time zone conflicts, add buffer time between calls, send reminders, and keep your schedule structured and conflict-free.

Data Entry & Reporting

I clean up and update databases and spreadsheets, standardize tags, organize cloud folders, and prepare summary reports.

File Management

I organize cloud storage folders, establish consistent naming conventions, archive outdated records, and structure documents so files are easy to find.

No-Code Website Setup

I set up simple websites and landing pages with embedded booking links and intake or feedback forms to establish a clean online presence and capture leads.

Content Moderation

I review submissions, user-generated content, posts, and appeals for policy compliance, escalate urgent cases, and track requests in a ticketing system to ensure platform safety and brand integrity.
